How GTU BBA Grading & Results Are Made

GTU Evaluation Scheme, Letter Grades, SPI Formula & Dynamic Rebalancing

Step 1

Evaluation Components & Credit Weightages

Each GTU BBA subject is evaluated out of 100% marks divided into 3 distinct components:

4-Credit Subjects (Max 150) PPM, FA, BSL, ENG
  • ESE University Exam: 70 Marks (Min 25/70 needed)
  • College Internal Practical / Project: 50 Marks (Unit 5 Project, Report & Viva)
  • College Internal Theory: 30 Marks (Mid-Sem 20 [Exam 40÷2] + Attendance 10)
2-Credit Subjects (Max 100) IKS, ESG / FES
  • ESE University Exam: 50 Marks (Min 18/50 needed)
  • College Internal Practical / Project: 20 Marks (Unit 3 Heritage / ESG Project & Viva)
  • College Internal Theory: 30 Marks (Mid-Sem 20 [Exam 40÷2] + Attendance 10)
📢 Official College Update · Mid-Sem Exam Evaluation

College conducts the internal Mid-Sem Exam out of 40 Marks. When reported to GTU, your marks are divided by 2 (scaled to 20 Marks).
Example: Scoring 40 / 40 in college exam = 20 / 20 GTU internal score (36/40 = 18/20, 30/40 = 15/20).

⚠️ Strict GTU Component-Wise Passing Rule: You must score at least 35% in EACH component head individually to pass the subject:
  • College Internal Theory: Min 11 / 30 (Mid-Sem 20 [40÷2] + Attendance 10)
  • College Practical / Project: Min 18 / 50 (4-credit) or 7 / 20 (2-credit)
  • GTU University Exam (ESE): Min 25 / 70 (4-credit) or 18 / 50 (2-credit)
  • Overall Aggregate Total: Min 35% (53 / 150 for 4-credit, 35 / 100 for 2-credit)
Critical: Scoring high in one component does NOT compensate for failing another. Failing any single head results in an FF Grade (0 GP / Backlog)!

Step 3

How Semester SPI & CGPA Are Calculated

📐 Semester Performance Index (SPI)
20 Credits
SPI =
∑ ( Ci × GPi )
∑ Ci (20)

4-Credit subjects carry double weight (4×) compared to 2-Credit subjects (2×). Scoring AA in a 4-credit subject gives 40 points.

📊 Cumulative CGPA Formula
All Semesters
CGPA =
Total Credit Points (All Sems)
Total Credits Attempted

CGPA measures cumulative academic performance by dividing total earned credit points by total credits across all semesters.
