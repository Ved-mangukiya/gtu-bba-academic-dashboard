How GTU BBA Grading & Results Are Made

GTU Evaluation Scheme, Letter Grades, SPI Formula & Dynamic Rebalancing

Step 1

Evaluation Components & Credit Weightages

Each GTU BBA subject is evaluated out of 100% marks divided into 3 distinct components:

4-Credit Subjects (Max 150) PPM, FA, BSL, ENG
  • ESE University Exam: 70 Marks (Min 25/70 needed)
  • Practical / Viva / Report: 50 Marks
  • College Internal: 30 Marks (Mid 20 + Att 5 + Beh 5)
2-Credit Subjects (Max 100) IKS, ESG
  • ESE University Exam: 50 Marks (Min 18/50 needed)
  • Practical / Activity: 20 Marks
  • College Internal: 30 Marks (Mid 20 + Att 5 + Beh 5)
⚠️ Mandatory GTU Passing Rule: A student must secure at least 35% in the ESE paper (25/70 in 4-credit, 18/50 in 2-credit) AND at least 35% aggregate overall to pass. Failing ESE marks directly awards an FF Grade (0 Grade Points).

Step 3

How Semester SPI & CGPA Are Calculated

Semester Performance Index (SPI) Formula:
$$\text{SPI} = \frac{\sum (\text{Subject Credits } C_i \times \text{Grade Point } GP_i)}{\sum \text{Total Semester Credits } (20)}$$

4-Credit subjects carry double the weight (4×) of 2-Credit subjects (2×). Scoring an AA (10 GP) in a 4-credit subject contributes 40 credit points, while in a 2-credit subject it contributes 20 points.
